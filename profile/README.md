# IEEE Intelligent Transportation Systems Society

## Nirma University Student Chapter

Welcome to the official GitHub organization of the **IEEE Intelligent Transportation Systems Society (ITSS) Student Chapter at Nirma University**, Ahmedabad, India.

---

## 📌 About Us

The **IEEE ITSS Student Chapter at Nirma University** is a student-run technical chapter affiliated with the globally recognized **IEEE Intelligent Transportation Systems Society**. Our community brings together students, researchers, and innovators passionate about shaping the future of mobility infrastructure, autonomous systems, and connected transportation.

We foster hands-on learning, engineering excellence, interdisciplinary research, and active collaboration across computer science, electronics, robotics, and mechanical engineering.

Through our chapter initiatives, members:

* Explore breakthroughs in autonomous vehicles, smart infrastructure, and connected transit.
* Design and build functional software and hardware prototypes.
* Attend curated expert lectures and technical talks from distinguished researchers and industry leaders.
* Compete in high-impact hackathons, codeathons, and mobility design challenges.
* Contribute to open-source transit solutions and technical repositories.
* Network with peer researchers, engineers, and global IEEE professionals.
* Develop technical, managerial, and collaborative leadership skills.

---

## 🚀 What We Do

### 🚗 Core Focus Areas

Our project groups and technical initiatives center around:

* **Autonomous Vehicles & ADAS:** Perception pipelines, sensor fusion, path planning, obstacle avoidance, and advanced driver assistance systems.
* **Connected Vehicles (V2X):** Vehicle-to-Vehicle (V2V), Vehicle-to-Infrastructure (V2I), and cooperative intelligent transit architectures.
* **Smart Cities & Urban Mobility:** Intelligent traffic signal networks, real-time fleet telematics, and transit infrastructure optimization.
* **Traffic Simulation & Modeling:** Congestion modeling, traffic flow algorithms, and synthetic transit network analysis.
* **Computer Vision in ITS:** Object detection, lane tracking, pedestrian recognition, and optical traffic monitoring.
* **IoT & Embedded Systems:** Edge computing, on-board sensor data acquisition, telematics units, and microcontroller interfacing.
* **Robotics & Autonomous Navigation:** Mobile robotics, SLAM (Simultaneous Localization and Mapping), and robotic transit platforms.

### 📢 Expert Lectures & Technical Talks

We regularly organize expert sessions, research talks, and interactive forums featuring prominent academicians and mobility industry pioneers, offering students deep insights into frontier research and real-world industrial ITS applications.

### 💻 Hands-on Workshops & Bootcamps

Practical, student-led technical workshops covering algorithmic fundamentals, embedded prototyping, data analytics, and software development applied directly to intelligent mobility.

### 🏆 Hackathons & Innovation Challenges

Dynamic problem-solving events where students build functional solutions to tackle real-world transit inefficiencies, commuter safety challenges, and urban mobility bottlenecks.

### 📂 Open Source & Collaborative Projects

We actively build and maintain software tools, web platforms, and research prototypes under this organization, encouraging open collaboration, peer review, and continuous technical contribution.

## 🎯 Our Vision

To cultivate an interdisciplinary, innovation-driven student ecosystem that actively researches, designs, and implements smart mobility solutions, shaping the future of autonomous systems and intelligent transit infrastructure.

---

## 🎯 Our Mission

* **Experiential Learning:** Bridge academic theory and real-world engineering through project-based learning in intelligent transportation.
* **Research Engagement:** Connect students directly with research frontiers through expert lectures, seminars, and technical discourse.
* **Open Collaboration:** Champion open-source principles and shared technical resources to solve pressing mobility and navigation challenges.
* **Interdisciplinary Synergy:** Encourage cross-disciplinary teamwork across computing, electronics, data, and robotics domains.
* **Industry & Academic Readiness:** Prepare students for advanced academic research and leadership careers in smart mobility and autonomous engineering.
